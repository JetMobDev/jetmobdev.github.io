Sound Machine

by JetMob · Privacy policy

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Sound Machine (APP) for mobile devices that was created by JetMob.
Application is available to download in Google Play (link to Application).

What information we collect?

Application does not collect personal information like name, surname, age, place of birth, and we do not ask you to create an account.

However, the third-party services listed below may automatically collect the following data from your device:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Usage data (e.g. ad interactions, app launches, device model, operating system version, language);
  • Diagnostics and crash data.

Purposes: showing ads (including personalised ads where you consent), measuring ad performance and app usage statistics, detecting and fixing errors, preventing fraud.

Sensitive permissions and features

We use the following features in our App that are deemed sensitive on Google Play:

  • VIBRATE
    Gives haptic feedback when you play a sound.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
    Lets you choose an audio file from your device. The file is not uploaded.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
    Same as above, on older Android versions.

Consent and how to withdraw it

Where required by law (e.g. in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the Privacy button in the app Settings, which re-opens the consent form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. Data stored by the app (settings, photos and records it creates) stays only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To delete advertising-related data, reset your advertising ID and withdraw your consent as described above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
